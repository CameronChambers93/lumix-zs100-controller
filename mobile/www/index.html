Settings

Camera

The app finds the camera on its own, also when your router gives it a new address. You only need this to force one. On the camera's own Wi-Fi it's always 192.168.54.1.

On the camera choose Wi-Fi, New connection, Remote Shooting & View, Direct: its screen shows the name and password. The first time, Android asks you to approve joining it (one tap). Only tried while the app is open and the camera isn't found on your current Wi-Fi.

Saving

New saves go here. Photos saved earlier to other folders still count as saved.

Location

The camera has no GPS. While this is on, the phone notes where it is every couple of minutes, and whenever you move 100 m, with a notification showing. Photos you save get the place they were taken, matched by time (within 10 minutes) using the camera's clock. The app can't set that clock, so check it on the camera (Setup menu, Clock Set).
